# Purchasing Policy

This document contains fictional rules for the Foundry Labs project.
It is not a real company policy.

## Approval thresholds

- Purchases up to and including BRL 5,000 require manager approval.
- Purchases above BRL 5,000 require director approval.

## Required information

Every purchase request must include:

- Item description.
- Quantity.
- Cost center.
- Business justification.

## Restrictions

- Available budget does not constitute purchase approval.
- This policy does not define supplier payment terms.
- This policy does not define international purchasing procedures.
